Our story

A label connected to the sound of modern Tanzania.

Founded by Tanzanian artist Diamond Platnumz, WCB Wasafi is a record label and entertainment company associated with the growth of Bongo Flava and East African music. Its story sits within a wider creative movement shaped by artists, producers, collaborators, audiences, languages, and the places that inspire them.

Music carries more than a melody. It can hold memory, ambition, celebration, and everyday life in the same verse. Tanzanian sounds continue to connect local expression with listeners across borders, while each artist brings a distinct voice and point of view.

Listening guide · 4 min

A first listen to Bongo Flava

Bongo Flava is a popular music style associated with Tanzania. Its sound is not fixed: artists draw on local languages and experiences while engaging with changing regional and global influences. Listen for the choices each artist makes rather than expecting one single formula.

Frequently asked questions

A few useful answers before you get in touch.

This independent page is an introduction, not an official representation of current roster, release schedules, booking availability, or commercial terms. Confirm current details with official WCB Wasafi and artist channels.

What is WCB Wasafi?

WCB Wasafi is a Tanzanian record label and entertainment company founded by artist Diamond Platnumz. This page provides a general introduction and is not an official label website.

Where can I find official songs and videos?

Use the featured search link on this page as a starting point, then verify the artist channel and release source before sharing or using music.

Can I send music for consideration?

Use the contact form to introduce yourself and include links to your work. A submission does not guarantee a reply, review, signing, collaboration, or other outcome.

Are prices, bookings, or partnerships guaranteed?

No. The enquiry cards are suggested ways to organize a request, not promises that a service is available. Confirm scope, availability, fees, and terms directly with the appropriate team.

What happens to my contact message?

If the contact form backend is configured, your message is stored for the team to review. Avoid sending passwords, financial details, private documents, or other sensitive information.

How do I unsubscribe from music updates?

Each newsletter message should include a working unsubscribe option. Newsletter delivery is not active until the site owner configures a mailing provider and an unsubscribe workflow.
